What to know before you hire

A little prep helps you compare quotes with confidence.

  • Ask about licensingRules vary by state and city. Ask the contractor what applies where you live.
  • Ask for a written quoteParts, labor, and any trip fee should be clear before work starts.
  • Note your door detailsBrand, age, size, and the symptoms help the pro bring the right parts.
  • Compare more than one offerYou are free to say no. You owe us nothing.

Stay safe with a broken door

Springs and cables hold strong tension. Do not try to adjust or remove them yourself. Keep people and pets away from a door that is stuck or hanging unevenly. Then request a pro.

Garage door referral questions

What is a garage door referral service?

We are a go-between. You tell us about your garage door problem. We send your request to an independent contractor who serves your area. The contractor then contacts you directly.

Does it cost anything to use this service?

No. Homeowners pay nothing to send a request. You only pay the contractor if you choose to hire them for the work.

Are the contractors your employees?

No. Every contractor is an independent business. We do not perform garage door work, and we do not control how contractors price or complete their jobs.

Do I have to hire the contractor who calls me?

No. You can ask questions, compare quotes, and say no at any time. There is no obligation.

Can I fix a broken garage door spring myself?

We advise against it. Garage door springs hold very high tension and can cause serious injury. Leave spring and cable repairs to a trained technician.

How quickly will a contractor contact me?

Response time depends on your area and the time of day. If your door is stuck open or you need urgent help, say so in your request.

What areas do you serve?

We match requests across the United States by ZIP code. Coverage varies by area, so enter your ZIP code to start.
